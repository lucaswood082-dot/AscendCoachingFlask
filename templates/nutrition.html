Nutrition Guide

Your complete guide to fueling performance, gaining muscle, or losing fat the right way.

1. Understanding Calories

Calories are your body’s energy source. - Eat more than you burn → weight gain - Eat less than you burn → fat loss - Eat around maintenance → maintain weight Consistency matters more than perfection.

2. Macronutrients

  • Protein: Builds muscle & keeps you full. Aim for 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight.
  • Carbs: Main energy source — especially for training.
  • Fats: Essential for hormones, brain function, recovery.

3. Example Daily Structure

  • Breakfast — Protein + carbs + fruit
  • Lunch — Lean protein + rice/pasta + vegetables
  • Snack — Yogurt, fruit, protein bar, nuts
  • Dinner — Protein + potatoes/rice + veggies

4. Hydration & Recovery

- 2–3L water per day - Extra on training days - Electrolytes optional but helpful

5. Supplements (Optional)

  • Whey protein — easy way to hit daily protein goals
  • Creatine — proven strength/performance boost
  • Multivitamin — fills basic gaps
  • Omega-3 — improves recovery & heart health

Final Advice

Nutrition doesn't need to be extreme. Build consistency, fuel your goals, and stay balanced.
