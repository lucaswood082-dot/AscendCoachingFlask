Supplements

Explore science-backed supplements that can support your training, recovery, and overall performance.

Whey Protein

Fast-digesting protein from dairy that supports muscle repair and growth. Integration: Take post-workout or between meals to hit protein targets.

Casein Protein

Slow-digesting dairy protein ideal for overnight recovery. Integration: Consume before bed for sustained amino acid release.

Plant Protein Blends (pea, soy, rice)

Vegan-friendly protein sources. Integration: Use to meet protein needs when avoiding animal products.

Creatine Monohydrate

Supports ATP production, strength, and power output. Integration: 3–5g daily, preferably post-workout.

Beta-Alanine

Buffers lactic acid, delaying fatigue in high-intensity exercise. Integration: 3–6g daily, split doses.

HMB (β-Hydroxy β-Methylbutyrate)

Reduces muscle breakdown and improves recovery. Integration: 3g daily, split throughout the day.

Omega-3 Fatty Acids (EPA/DHA)

Supports joint health, reduces inflammation, and aids recovery. Integration: 1–3g daily via fish oil or diet.

Citrulline Malate

Improves blood flow and reduces fatigue. Integration: 6–8g pre-workout for enhanced pump and endurance.

Branched-Chain Amino Acids (BCAAs)

Leucine, isoleucine, and valine promote muscle protein synthesis and reduce muscle soreness. Integration: Take around training if dietary protein is insufficient.

Glutamine

Supports gut health, immunity, and recovery. Integration: 5g post-workout or during periods of high stress.

Electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium)

Maintains hydration, nerve, and muscle function. Integration: Supplement during long workouts or heavy sweating.

Ashwagandha

Adaptogen that reduces stress and supports recovery. Integration: 300–600mg daily.

Rhodiola Rosea

Adaptogen that improves mental endurance and reduces fatigue. Integration: 200–400mg before workouts or stressful periods.

Phosphatidic Acid

Supports mTOR activation and muscle growth. Integration: 750mg–1g daily around workouts.

Ecdysterone (20-Hydroxyecdysterone)

Plant-derived compound that may enhance muscle mass. Integration: 200–500mg daily with meals.

Betaine Anhydrous

Supports strength, power, and body composition. Integration: 2.5g daily, preferably pre-workout.

L-Carnitine

Supports recovery and cellular energy production. Integration: 1–2g daily.

Collagen Peptides

Supports connective tissue and joint health. Integration: 10–20g daily, can mix in drinks or shakes.

Tart Cherry Extract

Reduces inflammation and supports recovery. Integration: 1–2g daily or as a concentrated juice.

Curcumin (Turmeric derivative)

Anti-inflammatory compound that supports recovery. Integration: 500–1000mg daily, ideally with black pepper for absorption.
